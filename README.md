## Is NoteLyn right for you?

Try NoteLyn if you want to:

- **Turn material you already have into a study session.** Start with a recording, reading, photo of notes or captioned YouTube lesson.
- **Practice what you need to remember.** Move from the summary to flashcards and quiz questions about the same material.
- **Work through a topic in different ways.** Read the note, explore its mind map and ask questions in the same workspace.

For your first try, choose a short recording or a PDF you know well. Check whether the summary captures the main ideas, the practice questions help you review, and the answers are useful when you get stuck.

## Create your first note in three steps

1. **Bring one source.** Open the [web app](https://app.notelyn.com/) or mobile app, sign in, and choose recording, upload or link import for your material.
2. **Review the note.** Read the summary and key concepts. Check important names, terms, numbers and conclusions against the original.
3. **Choose your next step.** Generate flashcards, take a quiz, explore a mind map or ask a question about the note. Save it in a notebook for next time.

## From a saved lecture to a review session

Picture coming back to a lecture recording before an exam. You want the main ideas, a way to check what you remember, and help with the parts that still feel unclear.

Here's a review routine you can build around a NoteLyn note:

1. **Get your bearings.** Read the summary and key concepts, then check important details against the recording or course material.
2. **Try answering from memory.** Generate flashcards and a multiple-choice quiz. Answer first, then read the explanations.
3. **Work through the gaps.** Ask AI about a confusing concept and use the mind map to see how it connects to the rest of the topic.

The same source becomes something you can read, question and practice with. [Browse a sample note](https://www.notelyn.com/samples/lecture) to see the format. The sample illustrates the experience; results depend on your material.

## Prompts and templates to try

These prompts are suggestions for the current note's AI chat. The templates are plain text you can copy into your own study log or meeting record.

### Understand a difficult concept

> Explain [concept] from this note in plain English. Give a simple example, then ask me one question to check my understanding.

### Prepare for a review session

> Suggest five questions I should be able to answer after studying this note. Focus on the main concepts and point me to the relevant sections.

### Clarify meeting follow-ups

> List the decisions and follow-up tasks explicitly mentioned in this note. Include an owner and deadline only when they were stated. Mark anything missing as "not specified."

Check the result against the source before relying on it or sharing it.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary><strong>How does NoteLyn compare with NotebookLM?</strong></summary>

NoteLyn turns a supported recording, document, image, text or link into an individual note with summaries, flashcards, quizzes and mind maps. [Google's Gemini Notebook / NotebookLM](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16179559?hl=en) lets you select notebook sources, ask questions and receive answers with source citations. It also offers [study tools](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16958963?hl=en). Compare source organization, checking answers against the material, and the review routine you prefer.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How does NoteLyn compare with Quizlet?</strong></summary>

NoteLyn combines recording and supported imports with structured notes, flashcards, quizzes, mind maps and questions about the current note. [Quizlet](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030841732-Studying-on-Quizlet) offers flashcard sets, study activities, and study guides and practice tests generated from course material. Both support practice; compare how you capture material, organize it and use it in a study session.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I try NoteLyn for free?</strong></summary>

Yes. [NoteLyn currently offers](https://www.notelyn.com/features) 2 free AI notes for new accounts, with no credit card required. Creating a note requires an account. Check the app for current allowances, paid plans and pricing; podcasts are a Pro feature that uses credits.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I import a YouTube video without captions?</strong></summary>

YouTube link import uses the video's captions, so videos without captions cannot be processed this way. If you have an audio file you can use, choose audio upload instead.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I combine a recording and slides in one note?</strong></summary>

Each note starts from one source. Create separate notes for the recording and slides, then organize them in the same notebook.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What does AI chat answer questions about?</strong></summary>

AI chat works with the note you have open. Ask it to explain a concept, compare ideas, clarify a passage or suggest review questions. Point to a specific term or section for a focused answer.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Do people need an account to open my shared note?</strong></summary>

No. A read-only link opens in the browser without signing in, and recipients cannot edit your original note. Check the content before sharing the link.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I export notes as files?</strong></summary>

Note file export is not currently available. You can share a note using a read-only link. Download options for individual content types are shown in the app.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Should I check the AI-generated content?</strong></summary>

Yes. Check names, terminology, numbers, dates and important conclusions against your source. For coursework, verify answers against your course materials.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does this repository include the app's source code?</strong></summary>

This repository contains guides, prompts and templates. NoteLyn's app and website source code are not included.

</details>
